Que.1. Where is Bandhavgarh National Park located?
Ans. It is located in the Umaria district of Madhya Pradesh, India, in the Vindhya hills.

Que.2. Why is Bandhavgarh National Park famous?
Ans. It is famous for its Bengal tigers, one of the highest tiger densities in India, and for the ancient Bandhavgarh Fort inside the park.

Que.3. Which is the best time to visit Bandhavgarh?
Ans. The best time is from October to March, when the weather is pleasant. The park remains closed for tourists in the monsoon season.

Que.4. How can I reach Bandhavgarh National Park?
Ans. The nearest major airport is Jabalpur, about 200 km away. Umaria and Katni are the nearby railway stations, and the rest of the journey can be done by road.

Que.5. How do I book a safari in Bandhavgarh?
Ans. Safaris are booked online through the official Madhya Pradesh forest department website. A jeep is hired for the safari and the entry fee and guide fee are paid separately.

Que.6. Which are the safari zones of Bandhavgarh?
Ans. The main safari zones are Tala, Magdhi and Khitauli. Tala is the most popular zone for tiger sightings.
